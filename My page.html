The Five Kings

No. Picture King Reign
1 Ugyen Wangchuck 1907 - 1926
2 Jigme Wangchuck 1926 - 1952
3 Jigme Dorji Wangchuck 1952 - 1972
4 Jigme Singye Wangchuck 1972 - 2006
5 Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck (current king) 2006 - present

Did you know?

  • The king of Bhutan is called the Druk Gyalpo, which means "Dragon King".
  • The first king, Ugyen Wangchuck, was elected by an assembly of his subjects in 1907.
  • The fourth king stepped down in 2006 so that his son could take the throne.
  • The fifth king was crowned on 6 November 2008, exactly 100 years after the monarchy began.
  • The king wears the Raven Crown.
  • Bhutanese people call themselves the Drukpa, "people of the Dragon".
  • Through the Kidu system, citizens can ask the king directly for help.

Short history

All five kings belong to the Wangchuck family. The first two kings built a strong central government. The third, fourth and fifth kings led Bhutan toward democracy and development. The fourth king is also linked with the idea of Gross National Happiness.
