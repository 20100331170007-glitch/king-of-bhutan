The Kings of Bhutan

Five Dragon Kings of the Wangchuck dynasty, from 1907 to today.

The King of Bhutan is called the Druk Gyalpo, meaning “Dragon King.” Every king so far has come from the Wangchuck family.

Ugyen Wangchuck

First king, 1907–1926

The hereditary Penlop of Trongsa. An assembly of his subjects elected him the first Druk Gyalpo in 1907, which began the dynasty.

Read more about Ugyen Wangchuck

Jigme Wangchuck

Second king, 1926–1952

His reign brought a stronger central government and the first steps in building roads and other infrastructure.

Read more about Jigme Wangchuck

Jigme Dorji Wangchuck

Third king, 1952–1972

He began the move toward democracy, decentralization and development, and is often called the father of modern Bhutan.

Read more about Jigme Dorji Wangchuck

Jigme Singye Wangchuck

Fourth king, 1972–2006

He continued the path to democracy and is linked with the idea of Gross National Happiness. In 2006 he stepped down in favor of his son.

Read more about Jigme Singye Wangchuck

Jigme Khesar Namgyel Wangchuck

Fifth king, 2006–present

Born on 21 February 1980. He was crowned on 6 November 2008, the hundredth anniversary of the monarchy. He is married to Queen Jetsun Pema, and the heir is Jigme Namgyel Wangchuck.

Good to know

  • The king wears the Raven Crown.
  • Bhutanese people call themselves the Drukpa, “people of the Dragon.”
  • Through the Kidu system, citizens can ask the king directly for help.
